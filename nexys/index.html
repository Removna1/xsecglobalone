Nexys

Executive Security. Managed continuously.

Nexys, is XSEC Global’s Travel Risk Management and Corporate Safety platform. Developed as a part of the global security operations capability, Nexys connects digital situational awareness, traveler safety and emergency support with XSEC’s international network of security resources on the ground.

The platform is designed to provide organizations and their people with continuous support before, during and after travel, while giving security teams improved visibility and the ability to respond rapidly when incidents occur.

Key Capabilities

24/7 Global Security Support

One-touch access to a 24/7 Global Security Operations Center, supported by specialists across security, medical, aviation and maritime operations.

Discreet Emergency Signaling

Silent Alert functionality enables users to request assistance discreetly when openly making a call or raising an alarm may increase their exposure.

Real-Time Situational Awareness

Live location capabilities, geofencing, automated check-ins and proximity-based alerts help identify when travelers or personnel may be affected by emerging incidents.

Global Ground Response

Nexys connects digital alerts with XSEC’s international response capability and partner network, enabling localized assistance, security response, evacuation support and medical evacuation when required.

Resilient Communications

Alternative and satellite-supported communication capabilities can provide additional resilience when conventional mobile or internet infrastructure is disrupted.

Integrated Corporate Safety

Organizations can maintain oversight of travelers, personnel and relevant incidents through one integrated security environment.

About XSEC Global

XSEC Group provides Executive Protection, secure transportation, risk management and corporate security services internationally. Through its own operational presence and global partner network, XSEC supports corporations, executives, families and UHNW individuals with both preventive risk management and rapid operational response.

Nexys extends this capability digitally, connecting intelligence, communication and physical response in one global security ecosystem.
