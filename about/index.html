About XSEC

Security built around people.

XSEC Global is an international security and risk management company specializing in Executive Protection and the security of individuals, families and organizations.

Our approach combines more than three decades of operational security experience with protective intelligence, technology, physical protection and a trusted international network.

But protection is only part of what we do.

We work closely with the people we protect, often becoming a trusted extension of their everyday life and operations. Our role is to understand what matters, anticipate what may happen next and quietly create the conditions for our clients to live, travel and operate with confidence.

Protecting freedom, mobility and operational continuity.

More than a security provider

A trusted security partner.

The best security relationships are built before they are urgently needed.

XSEC works as a long-term security partner to executives, families, corporations and organizations — providing access to advice, intelligence and operational capability whenever circumstances require it.

  • Sometimes that means deploying an Executive Protection team.
  • Sometimes it means changing a travel plan, investigating an emerging concern, reducing online exposure or quietly solving a problem before it reaches the client.
  • And sometimes the best protective decision is to do nothing at all.

The measure is not how much security we deploy. It is whether the right level of security is in place.

Our approach

Intelligence before intervention.

We believe effective security begins with understanding.

Before deploying resources, we assess the individual, exposure, environment, threat, consequences and operational requirements.

This allows us to apply proportionate measures and continuously adapt them as circumstances change.

Built on experience

On a journey since 1993.

XSEC Global was established in 2021, but the experience behind the company began almost three decades earlier.

Since 1993, our founder’s professional journey has been dedicated exclusively to security — from early operational assignments and investigations to international Executive Protection, corporate security and risk advisory.

That experience now forms part of a wider organization, combining experienced security professionals, specialists, technology and trusted partners across multiple countries.

Experience cannot be accelerated. It has to be earned.

Global reach. Local capability.

XSEC supports clients across borders through our own operational presence and a carefully developed international network.

This allows us to combine global coordination with local knowledge, personnel and resources — whether supporting an executive during international travel, protecting a family, managing an emerging situation or coordinating a complex multinational operation.

One relationship. Global capability.

Technology + People

Modern protection requires both.

Technology has fundamentally changed both the threats facing individuals and the way they can be protected.

XSEC combines experienced security professionals with proprietary and integrated technology designed to identify exposure earlier, maintain situational awareness and connect clients with assistance when required.

Together, these capabilities form an integrated protective ecosystem.
