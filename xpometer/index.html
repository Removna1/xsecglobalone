From information to intelligence

Every attack requires information.

  • Who is the person?
  • Where can they be found?
  • When will they be there?
  • How accessible are they?

The more information an adversary can obtain and connect, the easier it becomes to identify vulnerabilities, predict movements and find an opportunity.

Xpometer continuously measures this exposure through five weighted parameters:

Volume of Information
How much information exists?
Reach
How widely is it distributed and interacted with?
Accessibility
How easily can that information be connected to your identity, family, locations, assets and movements?
Threat Potential
Could the information trigger an antagonist, attacker or be exploited to cause harm?
Trend & Search Interest
Is attention increasing, when and where?

The result is a continuously updated Exposure Score, supported by alerts, analysis, predictive forecasts and forward-looking exposure intelligence.

From monitoring to foresight

Exposure Calendar

Exposure is not static. It changes with time, location and activity.

The Exposure Calendar identifies upcoming periods where public information, scheduled appearances, travel, events or increased attention may create elevated exposure. By connecting what is known about an individual with when and where it becomes relevant, Xpometer helps protective teams identify critical time windows before they occur.

Not just what is exposed. When it matters.

Travel Intelligence

Travel changes the risk environment.

Xpometer’s Travel Module combines the individual’s personal exposure profile with destinations, itineraries and travel plans to provide context-specific intelligence before and during a journey.

Travel information can easily be added through itineraries, documents or simple trip details, allowing Xpometer to assess how the individual’s existing exposure interacts with the destination, timing and planned movements.

This helps identify relevant exposure, threats and vulnerabilities before departure, allowing protective measures to be adapted before the journey begins.

Because the same journey can present very different risks depending on who is travelling.
